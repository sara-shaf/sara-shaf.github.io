About

Sara Shafiee builds AI that helps engineers design smarter. As Senior Researcher / Associate Professor at DTU's Department of Civil and Mechanical Engineering, she works where artificial intelligence meets complex engineering systems and digital manufacturing, turning decades of engineering knowledge into faster, better decisions.

As Principal Investigator, she leads externally funded research on agentic and multi-agent AI, recommendation systems for product design and configuration, explainable and constraint-driven models, LLM-based evaluation, and synthetic data for industrial applications, including projects funded by the Independent Research Fund Denmark. Her group, the Sara Shafiee Lab, combines fundamental research with close industry collaboration.

Her work has been recognised with the Agnes & Betzy Award, the Nordic Women in Tech Award, the Alexander Foss MADE Award, Innovation Fund Denmark's Industrial Researcher Prize, and best paper awards at IEEE and ICED. She is a member of CIRP, the International Academy for Production Engineering, and of the Young Academy of Technology, Science and Innovation (YATSI). Before academia she worked as an IT Project Manager and Senior Business Consultant at Topsoe, and she later founded and led DivERS as its CEO.
