Sara Shafiee's research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, complex engineering systems, and digital manufacturing — building computational models, machine learning methods, and simulation frameworks to support data-driven decision-making in product development and manufacturing.

She has led and contributed to funded interdisciplinary projects advancing the integration of AI and simulation in engineering design and production, with a growing focus on agent-based reasoning, explainable AI, and constraint-driven models for future engineering systems.
