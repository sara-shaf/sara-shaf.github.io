# Hi, I'm Sara Shafiee 👋

Senior Researcher at the [Technical University of Denmark (DTU)](https://www.dtu.dk), Department of Civil and Mechanical Engineering — Engineering Design and Manufacturing Systems.

My research sits at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, complex engineering systems, and digital manufacturing** — building computational models, ML methods, and simulation frameworks for data-driven decision-making in product development and manufacturing. Current focus: agent-based reasoning, explainable AI, and constraint-driven models for engineering systems.

### 🔬 Current work
- **RECODE** (Independent Research Fund Denmark) — PI: revolutionizing engineer-to-order companies with deep learning and advanced recommendation systems for product design
- Multi-agent LLM systems and agentic AI evaluation for manufacturing and engineering workflows
- Synthetic data generation methods for industrial/manufacturing settings
